Make gummyworm's output the most faithfully comprehensible rendering of a real image that a terminal can show. Measure first, then improve against the measurement.

Fidelity is judged two ways: an automatic score (render back with a known font, compare to the source with SSIM and Lab delta-E) and human recognition tests. Neither is optional; without both, 'best' is an opinion.
