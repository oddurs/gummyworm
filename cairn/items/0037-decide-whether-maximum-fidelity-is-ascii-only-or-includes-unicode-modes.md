Half-blocks, braille, sextants and octants carry far more information per cell than ASCII. Decide whether the goal covers strict ASCII only, or whether a Unicode mode is the maximum-accuracy mode with ASCII kept as a separate identity-preserving mode. Blocks the glyph-matching and subpixel items.
